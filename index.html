LOCAL LEARNING

Feedback, not surveillance.

The Reset Engine uses a small contextual-bandit score. It considers time, energy, place, silence needs and explicit “helped / did not help” feedback. It does not read messages, infer diagnoses or upload behavior.

BOUNDARIES

What MARGIN cannot know.

  • It cannot diagnose or predict a mental-health condition.
  • It cannot know whether an estimate or deadline is accurate.
  • It cannot replace emergency, medical or counseling support.
  • Imported files must be reviewed by the user.
